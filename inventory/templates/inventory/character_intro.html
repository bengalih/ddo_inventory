Welcome to DDO Inventory

Items don't have to belong to a character — you can add items right away and assign (or reassign) them to a character whenever you like, including later.

Characters are useful for one thing in particular: when someone searches and finds an item they need, knowing which character (and which server) has it tells them who to ask. If you'd rather set that up now, you can — or just skip it and dive in.
